# Subscription FAQs

## What subscription plans are available?
MediConnect offers three tiers:
- **Free**: Pay full doctor rate per visit. No monthly fee.
- **MediConnect Plus** ($19/month): 20% discount on all doctor visits, priority booking, chat follow-ups.
- **MediConnect Premium** ($39/month): 30% discount on all visits, 1 free GP visit per month, family sharing (up to 4 members), priority booking, chat follow-ups.

## How does the discount work?
When you book an appointment, the discount is applied automatically at checkout. For example, if a doctor charges $200 and you have Plus (20% off), you pay $160.

## Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes. Go to the Subscription page and select a different plan. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings > Subscription > Cancel Plan. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. No refund for remaining days. You can also cancel through the Manage Billing portal.

## What happens after I cancel?
You keep your discount and plan features until the end of your billing cycle. After that, you return to the Free tier and pay full doctor rates.

## What is the free GP visit on Premium?
Premium subscribers get 1 free general practitioner visit per month. This covers GPs, family medicine, internal medicine, and primary care doctors. Specialist visits are not included in the free visit but still get the 30% discount.

## How does family sharing work?
Premium subscribers can add up to 4 family members. Each family member gets the 30% discount on their own visits. Family members must have their own MediConnect account and be from the same household. You can change family members up to 2 times per year.

## How is my card charged?
Subscriptions renew automatically on the same date each month. You'll receive an email reminder before each charge. You can update your payment method through the Manage Billing portal.

## What if my payment fails?
We'll retry your payment over several days. During this time, your subscription remains active. If payment continues to fail, your subscription will be cancelled and you'll return to the Free tier.
