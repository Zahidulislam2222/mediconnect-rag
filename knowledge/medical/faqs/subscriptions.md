# Subscription FAQs

## Can I buy a subscription now?
Subscriptions, checkout and clinical benefits are unavailable in the current public showcase. Plan illustrations do not create an entitlement to appointments, discounts, priority care, free visits, chat follow-ups or family access.

## What plans and prices apply?
The project source contains configurable plan and entitlement logic. This FAQ does not establish live plan names, prices, discount rates, renewal charges or benefits. Any future offer must use approved current terms and server-verified entitlements; examples in a demonstration are not a purchase agreement.

## Can I upgrade, downgrade or cancel?
Real subscription management is unavailable through the public release. Proration, renewal dates, refunds, cancellation timing and failed-payment treatment are not established here. Do not assume a demonstration button or source implementation changes a real subscription.

## Is family sharing available?
Family sharing and other clinical subscription benefits are unavailable in the current release. Eligibility, household limits, provider availability and licensing conditions require verification before they can be offered.

## Where should I ask about a displayed plan?
Use the website's published project contact information. Do not send card details or patient information with the enquiry.
