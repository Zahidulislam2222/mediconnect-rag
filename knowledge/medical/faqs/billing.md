# Billing FAQs

## Can I pay for care through the current website?
Payment, checkout and clinical services are unavailable in the current public showcase. Do not enter card details or send payment to obtain a service shown in the demonstration.

## Does the project have payment integration?
The source includes Stripe billing and subscription integrations. Complete checkout, refund and subscription flows have not been verified for public use. Real payment availability and compliance are not established by this FAQ.

## What are the prices, accepted methods and refund terms?
No live consultation prices, accepted payment methods, cancellation fees, refund deadlines, tax treatment or insurance arrangements are established for the showcase. Example figures and source configuration are not an offer of care or a promise of reimbursement. Future payment services require published terms and verified application flows.

## Can I view billing history or update a card?
Real billing history, invoices and payment-method management are unavailable through the current public release. Contact the project's published support channel for project questions without sending card details or health information.
