# Billing FAQs

## How do payments work?
All payments are processed securely through Stripe. MediConnect never sees or stores your card number. Payments are encrypted and PCI-DSS compliant.

## What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) through Stripe.

## How do I get a refund?
Appointment cancellations made more than 2 hours before the scheduled time receive a full refund. Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days back to your original payment method.

## Where can I see my billing history?
Go to Billing to see all your past transactions, receipts, and payment history. You can also access detailed invoices through the Manage Billing portal.

## How do I update my payment method?
Click Manage Billing on the Subscription or Billing page. This opens Stripe's secure portal where you can update your card, view invoices, and manage payment methods.

## Do doctors have different prices?
Yes. Each doctor sets their own consultation fee based on their specialty and experience. General practitioners typically charge $50-$100, while specialists may charge $150-$350. Subscription discounts apply to all doctor rates.

## Is there insurance support?
MediConnect currently operates on a cash-pay model only. We do not process insurance claims. However, you may submit your receipt to your insurance provider for potential reimbursement depending on your plan.

## What currency are prices in?
All prices are displayed in US Dollars (USD). Applicable taxes may be added based on your location.
