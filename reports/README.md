# Security and verification reports

Reviewed 2026-10-13. Scan outputs are evidence for their exact source, rules, target and time. They do not certify legal compliance or prove the absence of vulnerabilities.

Keep credentials, private infrastructure identifiers, patient material and raw sensitive diagnostics out of public reports. Preserve original restricted evidence privately and publish only reviewed summaries with scope, findings, disposition and unavailable gates.

See [security architecture](https://github.com/Zahidulislam2222/mediconnect-infrastructure-production/blob/main/docs/SECURITY-ARCHITECTURE.md), [release status](https://github.com/Zahidulislam2222/mediconnect-infrastructure-production/blob/main/docs/RELEASE-STATUS.md) and the repository's [private reporting policy](../SECURITY.md).
